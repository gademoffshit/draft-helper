# Auto buy

Auto buy is off by default. Turn it on in the settings: **Auto buy > Buy the build automatically**; the gear next to it holds the details.

## What it does

* **Buys the plan.** The next item of the build is bought by parts as soon as you have the gold, in the order pros buy them.
* **Courier.** Brings items from the stash. Secret shop parts (Point Booster, Tiara, Ultimate Orb and others) are bought there by the courier.
* **Shop and stash.** When an item is out of stock, the next one is bought first. When the stash is full, auto buy waits for the courier.
* **Buyback.** In the late game part of your gold is kept for buyback.
* **Teleport.** Buys a TP scroll when you have none.
* **Inventory room.** When the slots are full, an old cheap item is sold or moved to the backpack, the way pros usually do it. A build item lying in the backpack is moved back into a main slot, at the base or when no enemy is near.
* **Disassemble.** When taking an item apart is cheaper than buying the parts again, it is disassembled.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Auto buy works during a match (in the hero demo too), but not on pause. Purchases go out as normal game orders with gaps between them.
{% endhint %}
